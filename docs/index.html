Never get blindsided by a quota limit.

BrainFuel is a tiny always-on-top desktop widget that watches your GLM Coding Plan quota — the 5-hour rolling window and the weekly allowance — so you always know how much you have left.

See your quota at a glance.

Two nested rings on a card that floats above your work. No browser tab to check, no surprise rate limits.

⭕

Nested quota rings

The outer ring tracks your weekly allowance, the inner ring tracks the 5-hour rolling window. Both animate as they drain and refill, so the state is readable in a quarter-second.

🔄

Auto-refresh

A clickable refresh button, plus automatic refresh every few minutes.

🎨

Warm and dark themes

Light / Dark / System, warm palette, with a card opacity slider so it never blocks your desktop.

🔔

Quota notifications

Optional desktop notification when a quota crosses an exhaustion threshold — default 80% used, configurable. So you wrap up before the wall, not after.

💬

Bilingual UI

Chinese / English toggle, system-locale aware.

📌

Movable & autostart

Drag the card anywhere — position is remembered. Optional start-on-login.
